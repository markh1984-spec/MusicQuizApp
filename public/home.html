A few questions

What do I actually need on the night?

A laptop and a projector or a screen the room can see, plus wifi or a mobile hotspot for phones to join on. Nothing is installed — it runs in a browser.

Can I write my own quizzes?

You can write and edit your own packs in the editor, and they are private to you. What you cannot do is have the app generate one for you — the catalogue is where written quizzes come from, and that is what the price includes.

What happens to my packs if I cancel?

Your account stops launching nights, and your own saved packs and past gigs stay on it. Come back and it all works again. Packs you bought outright stay bought.
