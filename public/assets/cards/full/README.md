# Whole cards

A file here is the **entire** card — your own ground, your own border. The app
draws no card under it and prints only the rank and suit **on top**, because at
the 45px a hand card gets on a phone, a supplied corner index is a smudge over
the one thing somebody is scanning for.

Named after the card's id, same as the folder above: `sk.png` is the King of
Spades. Portrait **5:7**, about 1024x1434, and **no letters or numbers
anywhere on it**.

Assumed **dark** — the rank prints light — and clipped to the card's rounded
corners, so whatever is in your file's four corners never shows.

**One file here dresses the whole deck.** Every card you have not supplied
takes a dark ground and a gold edge and keeps its own drawn pips, so twelve
court cards make a deck rather than twelve cards that look like they failed to
load. Nothing to switch on.
