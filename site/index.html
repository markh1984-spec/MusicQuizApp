What a night actually looks like

They scan and they are in

A code on the screen. No app to download, no account to make, no email address. Ten seconds and a team name.

Five kinds of round

General knowledge, whose face is this, name that intro, pick-them-all, and first letter only — where spelling does not count.

Music bingo too

Cards on their phones, you play a chorus and move on. Same room, completely different night.

Speed is the game

Ten points a second left on the clock, so the room answers rather than deliberates. Nobody googles at that pace.

Photos on the big screen

Send a picture from your phone with dog ears and a clown nose on it. It lands six feet wide, mid-quiz.

It survives a bad night

Pub wifi, a flat battery, a laptop restarting. Everything comes back where it was, with every score.
